    <h1>Artificial Intelligence</h1>
    <p>
        Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are 
        programmed to think and learn like humans. The term may also be applied to any machine that exhibits 
        traits associated with a human mind such as learning and problem-solving.
    </p>
    
    <h2>Types of AI</h2>
    <h3>Narrow AI (Weak AI)</h3>
    <p>Designed for a particular task. Virtual assistants like Siri and Alexa are examples of narrow AI.</p>
    
    <h3>General AI (Strong AI)</h3>
    <p>A theoretical form of AI where a machine would have intelligence equal to humans. It would have a self-aware consciousness with the ability to solve problems, learn, and plan for the future.</p>
    
    <h2>Current Applications</h2>
