    <h1>Renewable Energy</h1>
    <p>
        Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenishing but flow-limited. 
        They are virtually inexhaustible in duration but limited in the amount of energy that is 
        available per unit of time.
    </p>
    
    <h2>Types of Renewable Energy</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Solar Energy</strong> - Harnessing energy from the sun using photovoltaic cells</li>
        <li><strong>Wind Energy</strong> - Converting wind kinetic energy into electrical power</li>
        <li><strong>Hydroelectric</strong> - Generating electricity from flowing water</li>
        <li><strong>Geothermal</strong> - Using earth's internal heat for energy generation</li>
        <li><strong>Biomass</strong> - Converting organic materials into usable energy</li>
    </ul>
    
    <h2>Global Impact</h2>
    <p>The transition to renewable energy sources is crucial for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and combating climate change. Many countries have set ambitious targets for renewable energy adoption.</p>
