    <h1>Machine Learning</h1>
    <p>
        Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that provides systems the ability to 
        automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. 
        It focuses on the development of computer programs that can access data and use it to learn for themselves.
    </p>
    
    <h2>Types of Machine Learning</h2>
    <h3>Supervised Learning</h3>
    <p>The algorithm learns from labeled training data, making predictions based on that data. Examples include classification and regression tasks.</p>
    
    <h3>Unsupervised Learning</h3>
    <p>The algorithm learns patterns from unlabeled data. Common applications include clustering and dimensionality reduction.</p>
    
    <h3>Reinforcement Learning</h3>
    <p>An agent learns to make decisions by performing actions and receiving rewards or penalties. This approach is used in game playing and robotics.</p>
    
    <h2>Popular Algorithms</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Linear Regression</li>
        <li>Decision Trees</li>
        <li>Random Forests</li>
        <li>Neural Networks</li>
        <li>Support Vector Machines</li>
    </ul>
