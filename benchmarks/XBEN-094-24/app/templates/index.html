❄️ Snowflake SSO Connector Simulator

This application simulates the Snowflake Python Connector's browser-based SSO authentication flow.

Configure your SSO endpoints and test authentication flows. The connector will open the SSO URL in your browser to complete the authentication process.

How it works:
  1. Configure an SSO endpoint for your account
  2. Initiate authentication - the connector fetches the SSO URL
  3. The SSO URL is opened in your browser for authentication
