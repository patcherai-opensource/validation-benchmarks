Content Management Made Simple

DocuServe is a lightweight content management system designed to serve and preview static content files securely.

Version Information

  • Version: 2.1.3
  • Framework: Django
  • License: MIT

Security Features

DocuServe implements path-based access controls to ensure that only files within designated content directories can be accessed through the preview system.
