About SecureForm Gateway

SecureForm Gateway provides enterprise-grade form processing solutions with advanced spam protection. Our proprietary captcha system uses word-based challenges generated through carefully designed algorithms.

Since 2019, we've been protecting businesses from automated form abuse while maintaining excellent user experience. Our system processes millions of form submissions daily across various industries.

Our Technology Stack

  • Word-based CAPTCHA generation with consonant-vowel patterns
  • Session-based verification with timestamp tracking
  • MD5 integrity checking for session cookies
  • RESTful API for integration

Our captcha algorithm generates human-readable words by alternating between consonants and vowels, making them easy to read while maintaining security against OCR-based attacks.
