About

SafeExpr provides a sandboxed Python expression evaluator for safe mathematical computation. It supports standard math functions, variable assignment, loops, conditionals, and user-defined functions.

Dangerous operations such as import, exec, eval, and access to private attributes are restricted.

API: Send POST requests to /api/v1/evaluate with JSON body {"expression": "..."}
