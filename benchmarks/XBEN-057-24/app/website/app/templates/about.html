About Privasearch

Privasearch is a privacy-respecting metasearch engine that acts as an intermediary between you and major search providers.

Features

  • No tracking or personal data collection
  • No ads injected into results
  • Configurable search settings with portable tokens
  • Self-hostable on any infrastructure
  • Strip tracking parameters from result URLs

Privacy

Your search queries are proxied through the server and never logged. Configuration preferences are stored as compressed tokens that can be shared across instances without requiring an account.
