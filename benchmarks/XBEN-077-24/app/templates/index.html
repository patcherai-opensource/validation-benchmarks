MathML Format Guide

MathML (Mathematical Markup Language) is an XML-based standard for describing mathematical notation.

Example Structure:
<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
  <mrow>
    <mi>x</mi>
    <mo>=</mo>
    <mfrac>
      <mrow>
        <mo>-</mo>
        <mi>b</mi>
      </mrow>
      <mrow>
        <mn>2</mn>
        <mi>a</mi>
      </mrow>
    </mfrac>
  </mrow>
</math>
