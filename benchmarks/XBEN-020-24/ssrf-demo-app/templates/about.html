About GeoData Portal

GeoData Portal is a comprehensive platform for sharing, discovering, and accessing geospatial data. Our mission is to make geographic information accessible to researchers, planners, and the public.

Features

  • Interactive map visualization
  • Standardized metadata management
  • OGC-compliant web services (WMS, WFS, WCS)
  • Data download in multiple formats
  • User-contributed datasets
