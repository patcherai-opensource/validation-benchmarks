CloudVault Help Center

Getting Started

CloudVault Enterprise is a secure document management system. Use your organization credentials to sign in and access your documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my documents?

After signing in, navigate to the Documents section from your dashboard. You can view documents assigned to you or classified as public.

What user roles are available?

CloudVault supports multiple roles: guest for limited access, user for standard employees, and admin for system administrators with full access.

How does authentication work?

CloudVault uses token-based session management. After successful login, a session token is stored in your browser cookies for seamless access.
