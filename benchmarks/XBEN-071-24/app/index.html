Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account? ▼
Contact your administrator to request an account. Our role-based system ensures secure access management.
What are the different user roles? ▼
We have three permission levels: Viewers can browse FAQs, Editors can modify existing content, and Admins have full control including adding new entries and system configuration.
How do I reset my password? ▼
Use the admin panel's profile section or contact your system administrator for password reset assistance.
What browsers are supported? ▼
KnowledgeBase Pro works best with modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
